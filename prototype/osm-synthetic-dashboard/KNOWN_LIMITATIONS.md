# Known limitations

- Delivery times are estimates from a route multiplier, assumed speeds, preparation and handoff. They are not observed deliveries or statistical intervals.
- Transit Stress Index measures modeled travel against a budget. It does not validate cancellation probability. The historical index uses a different definition.
- Low, Base and High order pools are hypothetical and allocated equally. The history uses its original fixed modeled demand.
- Only submitted student reports contribute actual feedback. A public installation starts with none. Small samples describe those reports.
- Restaurant menu-price evidence is unavailable. Operating status may be unknown and directory coverage is incomplete.
- Automated OSM refresh is disabled. The public-facing site should be staged with HTTPS and Turnstile before reports are accepted.
