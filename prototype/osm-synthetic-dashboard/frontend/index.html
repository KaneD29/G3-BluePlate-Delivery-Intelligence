Privacy & Data Use

Guest feedback helps BluePlate summarize delivery experiences. Reports are voluntary, and a small sample cannot establish a restaurant’s overall performance.

The public feed and API can show the restaurant and zone, outcome, delivery time, rating, submission date and time, report type, and report reference. Quick notes and written details are stored with the report and omitted from the public feed and dashboard. Leave out personal information. If your restaurant is missing, its submitted name and location stay private while the team reviews the listing.
