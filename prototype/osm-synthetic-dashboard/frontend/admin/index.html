Data & Methodology
  • Restaurant inventory. Venue names and locations come from OpenStreetMap (ODbL). Address and cuisine coverage depend on the available tags. OSM attribution
  • Modeled warehouse estimates. ETA combines estimated travel time, cuisine-based preparation time, and four minutes for handoff. Demand assumes 25 orders per restaurant per day. These estimates are separate from actual orders and student reports. The legacy cancellation index is 2.5 + 2.2 × straight-line distance in kilometers, expressed in points. BluePlate does not observe live delivery-platform transactions. The historical menu price column shows modeled averages when available. The warehouse currently has no documented menu price snapshots.
  • Current scenario snapshot. Straight-line Haversine distance from UST sets bands A (0–500 m), B (over 500–1,000 m), and C (over 1,000–1,500 m). Street and neighborhood filters are independent address labels. ETA uses a 1.35 route factor, 18 or 12 km/h rider speeds, 7 or 15 minutes of preparation and a four-minute handoff. Transit Stress Index compares conservative modeled travel time with a 15-minute budget. Demand is a hypothetical daily pool allocated equally. Current pricing, general delivery risk and cancellation probability are unavailable. The historical date uses the original fixed 25-order assumption.
  • Warehouse ratings. The sample count is the number of dates with an eligible warehouse rating for that restaurant. A date qualifies when it has a review count above zero and a rating from 1 to 5.
  • Self-reported observations. Student reports are a self-selected sample. Small samples describe those reports only. Completed-delivery averages use completed reports with valid minutes. Self-reported cancellation share is cancelled reports divided by reports with a known outcome. A zero denominator gives an unavailable share. Report dates are Manila submission dates. Reports for unlisted restaurants stay outside restaurant and zone analytics until matched to an approved venue within 1,500 meters of UST. The pending count is separate. Matching a report does not create modeled data.
  • Dates and reloads. The warehouse date identifies the data snapshot. The latest guest date comes from the 50 most recent public reports. The Latest report date filter includes all stored submission types. The view-loaded time is when your browser received the dashboard data. Reload dashboard data requests another view of existing records. ETL runs separately, and its execution time is unavailable on this dashboard.
